# Decisions and alternatives

One row per decision. Ask "why X instead of Y" and the answer should be here.
Status: **proposed** (my pick, not yet confirmed by the owner), **decided**, **open** (waiting on research or the owner).

## Goal (user, 2026-11-18)

An open-source app that gets the most information out of EEG electrodes, to make life better:
sleep tracking, stress / anxiety / focus, "take a break" hints while coding, generative music,
lots of statistics on how things affect the owner. Phone matters at least as much as the PC
(put the headband on, tap one button, sleep). Device order: BrainBit Black first, then the DIY
ADS1299 board (8-16 electrodes, any positions), then others such as OpenBCI.

## Facts measured on this laptop (2026-11-18, Linux Mint 22.3)

- BrainBit Black (firmware 1.10) connects from
  Python with BrainBit's pyneurosdk2 1.0.15: 4 channels O1 O2 T3 T4, 250 Hz, battery read,
  contact check (resistance) works. No pairing needed. No motion sensor or pulse sensor reported.
- Not yet tested on a head: the readings above were with the headband off (contacts = infinity).
- Setup without sudo: `libneurosdk2.so` (from BrainBit's Ubuntu 24.04 package, 1.0.6.39) plus
  libc++ / libc++abi / libunwind 18 unpacked into `~/.local/lib/neurosdk2`; run with
  `LD_LIBRARY_PATH=~/.local/lib/neurosdk2`. Probe: `scripts/brainbit_probe.py`.

- `scripts/export_events.py`: `eeg_sleep_night` events in utof_tracker's envelope (docs/data-format.md); a separate
  type from utof_tracker's own `sleep_night`, so neither overwrites the other. utof_tracker does not read them yet.
- `scripts/import_phone.py`: the phone app's Export data folder onto the laptop, every file checked against the
  export's SHA-256 list; raw nights into `_local/recordings/`, the rest into `_local/phone/` (rows 150-157).
- `analysis/lucid_cues.py`, `analysis/lucid_signals.py`, `analysis/make_lucid_fixture.py`: the lucid dream cues' rule
  and dream-signal finder (references for `app/lib/lucid/`), their replay and false-signal count on a recorded night,
  and the Dart port's fixture (rows 300-309).
- `scripts/phone_night_check.py`: how a night recorded by the phone went, in one command: every gap with its
  reason, lost packets per hour, sample rate, drops and reconnects, restarts by Android, batteries, contact,
  signal sanity, the phone's sleep report, a verdict and the top 3 fixes; `--compare` puts a laptop night
  beside it (row 320).
- `analysis/two_pass.py`: the phone's two-pass sleep scorer in Python (the reference for `app/lib/sleep/model.dart`,
  rows 260-264); `score_sleep.py` uses it for nights over 14 h, and `two_pass.py <night.bdf>` compares a night's
  stages three ways against the laptop's report (rows 310-312).
- `scripts/catalog.py`: a catalog of every night on the laptop (laptop and phone nights: files, hours, missing
  minutes, the report's asleep / deep / dream minutes, check-in) with the SHA-256 of every file in
  `_local/catalog/`; says what grew, changed or went missing since the last run; `--check` re-hashes everything
  (rows 313-318).
- `scripts/diy_board.py`, `scripts/record_diy.py`: the laptop host for the DIY ADS1299 board (its "E9" frames over
  USB serial or Wi-Fi UDP): `DRIVER=diy scripts/record_night.sh` records it into `_local/recordings-diy/` with the
  same files and live pages; tested on a fake board (`tests/fake_diy/`) and on frames from the firmware's own code,
  not yet on the board (rows 350-359, [docs/diy-board.md](docs/diy-board.md)).
