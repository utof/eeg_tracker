alpha at the back of the head (O1, O2) · the line is where "closed" starts
  • High beep: eyes open. Low beep: eyes closed.
  • Calibrate once: 10 s eyes open, then 10 s eyes closed when you hear the low tone. Sit still, relax the jaw.
  • It works from alpha waves (8-12 Hz), which rise at the back of the head when the eyes close. Some people show little alpha; then the two states look alike.
