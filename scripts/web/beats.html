Keep the volume low. Not while driving. Binaural beats need headphones; the other kinds work on a speaker. The hearing part of the brain reliably follows pulses; whether that moves your whole state is not proven, and plain calm sound often does as well. Use the no-beat option on some days to see what works for you.
