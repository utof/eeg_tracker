Brain sound

Rain and a low drone. Close your eyes and settle. When the alpha rhythm at the back of your head rises above your usual eyes-closed level, the rain softens and the drone warms; stay there a few seconds and a bell rings. You don't need to make it happen: just notice what you feel when the rain softens. A tight jaw or a blink pauses it; if you start drifting off, the sound gets a little brighter.

Press start. Headphones, low volume.

A relaxing tool, not a treatment. Alpha feedback helps some people feel calmer, but in careful trials it often does no better than sham feedback. Four dry electrodes see the back-of-head alpha rhythm and jaw muscle well; they cannot read stress, mood or focus.
