# Sleep experiments (what does late caffeine, a late workout or a few drinks do to your sleep?)

## What it does

In the phone app (not yet run on a phone). You start one experiment and tap once each evening whether the thing
happened. The app compares your nights with it and your nights without it, and says "too early to say" until there
are enough nights.

## Evidence for this headband: some

The effects of a lot of late caffeine, a very hard late workout and three or more drinks are well measured in
studies. Whether they show in your nights depends on how much your own nights vary, and the sleep numbers and the
pulse from this headband are a first guess that has not been checked against a lab or a watch.

## What the research says

- **A lot of caffeine, late.** In a trial with 23 men, 400 mg of caffeine (about 4 cups of coffee) four hours
  before bed meant 51 minutes less sleep, sleep efficiency 9.5 points lower, 26 minutes more awake in the night and
  34 minutes less deep sleep. The same dose 8 hours before bed still cost sleep. People hardly noticed the loss
  themselves, which is the reason to measure it.
- **One cup changes nothing you could see.** In the same trial 100 mg (one cup) 4, 8 or 12 hours before bed showed
  no clear change. The app does not offer that experiment.
- **A very hard workout, late.** In 4 million nights of 14,689 wristband wearers, a long, hard session that ended 2
  hours before the usual sleep time went with sleep starting 36 minutes later, 22 minutes less sleep and a pulse
  while asleep 3.9 beats a minute higher than after a light one. Finished 4 hours or more before bed: no difference.
  An ordinary evening gym visit showed no harm in lab trials, so the app does not offer it.
- **Alcohol.** The surest sign is the pulse while asleep: about 1.2 beats a minute higher per drink in 20,968
  wristband wearers, and 63.6 against 66.6 beats a minute after evenings of 4 to 6 drinks in another study. In 27
  controlled studies dream sleep started 18 minutes later and was 11 minutes shorter on average, from about 3
  drinks up. Total sleep, wake-ups and deep sleep did not change reliably.
- **One or two drinks** move the pulse by about 0.5 to 1.2 beats a minute each. Against the usual night-to-night
  spread that would need a hundred nights or more, so those nights are left out.
- **Late dinners and evening screens** are not offered. The one good lab trial of a late dinner found no clear
  harm, and screens change the time to fall asleep by minutes at most: both would need 60 nights or more each way
  and would most likely end in "no difference seen".
- **How many nights.** Nights needed of each kind are about 16 divided by the square of (the expected change
  divided by your own night-to-night spread), plus a quarter because one night resembles the next. For the three
  experiments here that comes to about 8 to 15 nights each way for caffeine, 10 to 16 for the workout and 10 to 25
  for three or more drinks. In a typical person total sleep varies by 67 minutes from night to night and sleep
  efficiency by 6.5 points.

## Where the data does not support it

- When you choose the nights yourself, the nights with and without differ in other ways too: a late night out, a
  day off, a big dinner. The app can then only say "seen together", never "caused".
- The trials were on other people: young men who drink coffee often, or wristband wearers. You may react more or
  less.
- This headband under-counts dream sleep, so less dream sleep after alcohol may not show here even when it happens.
- The pulse is read from the headband's own signal and is not yet checked against a watch.
- The workout figures come from a wristband that cannot tell "fell asleep later" from "went to bed later".
- One result is wrong about 1 time in 20 even when nothing is there, and looking every day makes that a little
  worse. Run it again before you rely on it.
- This is general information, not medical advice. The app never tells you to drink.

## How the app uses this

- Trends, then Sleep experiments. One at a time, from a short list: **Late caffeine**, **Late hard workout**,
  **Alcohol**.
- Each evening one tap out of three. Caffeine: "A lot, late", "None after noon", "In between". Workout: "Late",
  "Early", "None or light". Alcohol: "3 or more", "None", "1 or 2". The middle kind of evening goes in neither pile,
  because the studies say it is too small to show.
- The answer is judged by the night that follows, from the sleep report the app makes. It needs no channel names,
  so it works with any headband layout. A night you removed or marked "Not sleep", a night too short to score, one
  somebody else wore and one read mostly through noise do not count.
- Also left out: a night you ticked "Ill" or "Not my own bed", the night after a night under 5 hours, and for
  alcohol the night after 3 or more drinks (the pulse is still raised).
- What it watches. Caffeine: sleep efficiency (the main number), deep sleep, time awake in the night. Workout:
  pulse while asleep, the time sleep began. Alcohol: pulse while asleep, dream sleep.
- Under 5 nights of a kind it shows only the counts. From 5 of each it shows the two averages and "too early to
  say". A verdict comes only from the nights needed, worked out from your own spread once you have 14 ordinary
  nights (other people's until then), and never before 10 of each.
- The verdict is a shuffle test: the same difference with the labels dealt again at random (every way up to
  200,000, else 20,000 random ones). The range is every size of effect that test does not rule out at 5 %. "No
  difference seen" also says how small an effect this many nights cannot show.
- Beside it: the same difference among similar nights only (work days and days off apart, sleep within an hour of
  the usual time). When that gap is less than half the plain one the app says part of it may be late nights.
- For caffeine and the workout you can let the app pick: on an evening you are free to do either, you ask and it
  says with or without by chance (blocks of 4 test nights, 2 of each). Only then does a clear result say "Tested".
  It is off unless you switch it on, and every picked night counts as picked whatever you did.
- Every tap is a line in a file on the phone that leaves with Export data and Share on Wi-Fi like everything else.
